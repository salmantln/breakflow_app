About Break Flow

Break Flow

Version 1.0.0

Break Flow is a smart break reminder app that helps you maintain a healthy work-life balance with intelligent meeting detection, auto-pause, and calming break overlays.

Features

  • Universal meeting detection (Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack, Discord)
  • Auto-pause during meetings, fullscreen, and recording
  • Calming break overlays with exercises
  • Focus app detection
  • Floating countdown widget
  • Posture reminders
  • Global keyboard shortcuts
